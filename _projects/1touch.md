## Introduction

Soccer fans see the same basic numbers everywhere: goals, assists, and possession. These statistics rarely explain *why* a player or team performs well, and most apps show every user the same feed.

1touch is a mobile app that gives each fan a **personalized** set of statistics and **new metrics** that evaluate players and teams more meaningfully.

## Design

As **product manager**, I:

- **defined the project scope** and decided which features belong in the first release;
- **designed the overall structure of the app**, including its screens and how data flows between them;
- **designed new metrics** that are both interesting to fans and useful for evaluating players and teams.
