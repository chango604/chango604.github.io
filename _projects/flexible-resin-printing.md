## Introduction

Soft robots and wearable sensors often use 3D-printed flexible parts. Their stiffness, however, depends strongly on how they are printed and cured; two parts printed from the same resin can behave very differently. Without a reliable process, designers waste time and material on parts that are too stiff or too soft.

My objective was to find **print and post-curing settings** that give flexible resin parts a **predictable, desired stiffness**.

## Methods

I varied the 3D-printing settings and the post-curing process for a flexible photopolymer resin. For each condition, I printed **standard dogbone specimens** and measured their elastic modulus with tensile tests.
