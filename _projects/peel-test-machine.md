## Introduction

Labs that develop new adhesives, including the Soft Materials and Structures Lab, need reliable peel data to compare materials. In a 90° peel test, the tape must leave the surface at exactly 90° and at a constant speed; if the angle drifts, the measured force no longer reflects the adhesive alone.

Our team's objective was to build a **continuous 90° peel-test machine** that keeps both the angle and the speed constant for the whole test.

## Design

The machine uses two coordinated motions. A vertical axis pulls the tape upward through a force sensor, while a horizontal stage moves the substrate at the same speed so the peel point stays directly below the pull.

**My contribution** was the electrical system. I:

- designed and built the circuit that **powers both motors** and **controls their speeds** so the two axes stay matched;
- created the team's **Gantt chart** and a **budget** that covered the full academic year.
