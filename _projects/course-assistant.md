## Introduction

Mechatronics is often the first course in which mechanical engineering students must make circuits, motors, sensors, and code work together. When a system fails, students struggle to tell whether the problem is in the wiring, the code, or the mechanics.

As a course assistant, my objective is to help students **find and fix those problems themselves**, so they leave the course able to debug real systems.

## What I do

- **Mechatronics Applications in Mechanical Engineering (Fall 2025).** I support students in lab and office hours as they build projects with microcontrollers, sensors, and motors.
- **Mechanics I: 2D Design (Fall 2026).** I help students set up free-body diagrams and solve 2D statics and design problems.
