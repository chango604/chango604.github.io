## Introduction

Many students never meet an engineer before choosing a college major, and students from underrepresented groups are the least likely to have that chance. Outreach programs help close this gap by letting students try engineering for themselves.

I led activities for two programs: **Black Engineering Excellence** (July 2024) and **Pathways for Future Engineers** (June 2025).

## What I did

As activity lead, I planned a hands-on engineering activity, prepared the materials, and guided student groups as they built and tested their designs.
